Rollplayer Privacy Policy

last updated: May 7th, 2026

Rollplayer does not store any personal information - however, it can store options & preferences as defined by you, as well as the commands you use.
Some of this is automatically anonymized by its purpose - random text strings, for example. However, user settings aren't anonymized.
This data is not deleted automatically, since it's not personal information (and, you probably want to keep your preferences anyway)
We also store command data about you, which is manually cleared around once a year.
This data is stored in the United States.

This policy page may be changed at any time, however you are entitled to be informed through the "ACC's Miscellanea" discord server, in the "roll-updates" channel.
If you want your information deleted, contact @whirlingstars on Discord.
